LaunchOS
Design reference · fictional product · English-only scope
In plain words: one launch brief in — what ships, for whom, when. LaunchOS sets the tier (T1/T2/T3), builds the timeline and readiness gate, and generates the whole kit: release notes, blog, email, posts, sales one-pager, FAQ. Everything about the launch lives at one URL.
The twist: it doesn't just generate — it checks that every asset still matches the brief. A pricing claim that got stronger in the email is flagged before launch, not discovered by legal after. And when the brief changes, drifted assets go stale automatically.
